## 🔒 Data Anonymization Notice

All personally identifiable information (PII)—including policyholder names, policy certificate numbers, chassis identifiers, and license plate numbers—has been replaced with synthetic test values (e.g. `TARO YAMADA さま`, `品川300 さ 1234`, `POL-SAMPLE-202501`) to protect privacy while preserving 100% technical fidelity, legal article numbers, and market pricing realities.
