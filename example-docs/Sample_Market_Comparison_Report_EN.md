## Section 3: Switching Action Plan & Strategic Considerations

### 1. NCD Grade Portability (等級の引継ぎ)
Under Japanese automobile insurance regulations set by GIROJ, **your Non-Fleet Grade (8等級) is 100% legally portable across all domestic insurers**. If you complete your 1-year policy period without claimable accidents, you will advance to **9th Grade (9等級)** on the new policy, earning an additional ~2–3% premium discount.

---

### 2. Mid-Term Cancellation vs. Renewal Switching (中途解約 vs 満期切り替え)

#### ⚠️ The Mid-Term Cancellation Pitfall: Short-Term Rate Tables (短期率)
If you cancel an auto insurance contract prior to its expiration date (mid-term), Japanese insurers do **not** refund your unearned premium on a pro-rata daily basis. Instead, they apply a **Short-Term Rate Schedule (*短期率*)**, retaining a higher percentage of the premium. Furthermore, switching mid-term resets your 1-year grade advancement cycle, delaying your promotion to Grade 9.

#### ✅ The Optimal Switching Strategy: Switch at Renewal (満期切り替え)
- **Target Date:** **October 23, 2026 at 4:00 PM**
- **Financial Benefit:** Zero penalty, 100% pro-rata value extracted from current insurer, clean promotion to Grade 9, and maximum eligibility for competitors' "Early Bird" (*早割*) and "New Customer Online" discounts (up to ¥20,000).
